##Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
1. How do I purchase a ticket on AfriqTickets?

To purchase a ticket, browse available events on the AfriqTickets platform, select the event you wish to attend, choose your preferred ticket type, and proceed to checkout. After completing the payment, your ticket will be sent to your email or available in your account.

2. How will I receive my ticket?

Once your purchase is successful, you will receive a digital ticket via email or through your AfriqTickets account. The ticket may include a QR code or unique ticket number which will be scanned at the event entrance.

3. Can I transfer my ticket to someone else?

Ticket transfer depends on the policies set by the event organizer. Some events may allow transfers while others may not. Please review the event details before purchasing.

4. What payment methods are accepted?

AfriqTickets supports multiple payment methods depending on availability, which may include mobile money, debit cards, credit cards, or other digital payment services.

5. What happens if an event is canceled or postponed?

If an event is canceled or postponed, AfriqTickets will notify ticket holders via email or SMS with instructions regarding refunds, rescheduling, or ticket validity.

6. Can I request a refund for my ticket?

Refund policies are determined by the event organizer. Please refer to the Refund & Cancellation Policy or the specific event terms before requesting a refund.

7. How can I contact customer support?

If you need assistance, you can contact AfriqTickets customer support through the Contact Us page or via email at support@afriqtickets.com
