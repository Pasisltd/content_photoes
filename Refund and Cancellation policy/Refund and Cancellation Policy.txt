##Refund & Cancellation Policy
Refunds

Refunds for tickets purchased through AfriqTickets are subject to the policies set by the event organizer. Some events may allow refunds within a specific period, while others may have a no-refund policy. Users are encouraged to review the event details carefully before completing their purchase.

Event Cancellation

If an event is canceled by the organizer, ticket holders may be eligible for a full refund or ticket transfer depending on the organizer’s decision. AfriqTickets will notify all affected customers with further instructions.

Event Postponement or Rescheduling

If an event is postponed or rescheduled, tickets will generally remain valid for the new event date. If you are unable to attend the rescheduled event, you may contact the organizer or AfriqTickets support for available options.

Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed using the original payment method used during purchase. Processing time may vary depending on the payment provider.

Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds may not be available in the following situations:

If the event organizer has specified a no-refund policy

If the ticket holder fails to attend the event

If the refund request is submitted after the allowed refund period
